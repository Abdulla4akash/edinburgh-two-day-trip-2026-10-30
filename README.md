# Edinburgh two day itinerary

**Friday 30 and Saturday 31 October 2026. All times are Edinburgh local time, GMT.**

Arrive at **Edinburgh Waverley at 11:31am on Friday**, with no luggage. Go straight sightseeing, then reach the Airbnb **after 11pm**. Saturday ends at **Waverley by 7:30pm**, allowing about 30 minutes before the roughly **8pm departure** you confirmed. Check the exact departure on your ticket.

Keep the accommodation address and host check-in instructions separately; they are omitted from this public itinerary.

This is a walking-focused first visit: castle and Old Town today; Dean Village, Stockbridge and the museum tomorrow. Allow roughly **8–11km walking each day**, depending on detours and transport. Distances, walking times and spending allowances below are planning estimates. Use a bus or taxi to shorten the longer transfers.

**Chosen plan:** follow the Friday and Saturday tables below, including **Dean Village and Stockbridge on Saturday morning**, then the museum and an early dinner. Arthur's Seat is an optional substitute, not part of the chosen route.

This is **Codex's recommended itinerary**, updated after comparing [Claude's alternative](CLAUDE-PLAN.md). Read the [comparison and tradeoffs](COMPARISON.md).

**If you skip the ghost tour:** enjoy the Royal Mile after dark and have a longer dinner or indoor break. For a quieter early-evening option, Knoops at 106 George Street lists Friday/Saturday closing at 9:30pm; it will not cover the whole wait until 11pm. If Brewhemia is full or unsuitable, arrange another late-opening indoor venue while you are in town rather than counting on cafés staying open. [Knoops](https://knoops.com/pages/store-locations/edinburgh-new-town).

**Late return:** leave after 11pm and use a taxi or a live walking/transit route to the exact address supplied by your host. Confirm your late-check-in/key instructions before setting off.

## Saturday alternative for Arthur's Seat and Holyrood

Claude's strongest addition is a morning on Arthur's Seat followed by Holyrood. Choose this **instead of Dean Village, Stockbridge and Circus Lane** if a hill walk is a priority. Keep the afternoon museum and the 7:30pm Waverley target.

| Time | Alternative morning |

| 9:30am–12:00pm | Arthur's Seat ascent, views and descent, allowing about 2–2.5 hours for the full walk. This is a planning allowance, dependent on route, fitness and conditions. Turn back early if needed rather than rushing. |

Check current path closures on [Historic Environment Scotland's Holyrood Park page](https://www.historicenvironment.scot/visit/all/holyrood-park/) and follow local signs. If conditions are poor, use the original plan or its indoor alternative. Do not add the western neighbourhood loop afterwards: enjoy the museum and a relaxed dinner before the train.

## Weather and Halloween

The Met Office regional forecast checked on 30 October describes Friday as largely dry with sunny periods, and Saturday as becoming brighter after early cloud/patchy rain, with more cloud and light rain later. These are regional conditions, not a promise for each stop. Bring a warm layer and waterproof jacket. [Live Edinburgh forecast](https://weather.metoffice.gov.uk/forecast/gcvwr3zrw).

Plan outdoor photographs before **about 4:30pm**. Published sunset estimates are around **4:40pm Friday and 4:38pm Saturday**. [Daylight table](https://www.metcheck.com/OTHER/suntimes.asp?Location=Edinburgh&LocationID=685491&lat=55.95206&lon=-3.19648).

**Wet-weather swap:** replace Saturday's Dean Village/river walk with the museum from 10am, followed by a long lunch and shops/cafés. Keep the remaining Old Town visits flexible. If Friday is wet, use the castle alternative and skip the hill. There is no need to force an exposed viewpoint in poor conditions.

**Samhuinn is excluded:** the organiser lists the 31 October festival as sold out, with gates at 6:30pm and the show ending around 10:30pm. It does not fit your 8pm departure. Its festival page contains an inconsistent weekday label; the organiser's separate 2026 announcement correctly identifies 31 October as Saturday. Calton Hill access may be affected by preparation, so Friday's viewpoint remains conditional. [Festival details](https://beltane.org/samhuinn-fire-festival-2026/), [2026 announcement](https://beltane.org/2026/).
