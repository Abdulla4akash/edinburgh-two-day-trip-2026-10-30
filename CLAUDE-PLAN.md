# Claude plan — Edinburgh, Fri 30 – Sat 31 October 2026

Alternative itinerary written by Claude. `README.md` remains the canonical itinerary.
All times are suggestions; nothing here is booked. Recheck hours, fares, weather and tickets.

## Key facts

- Arrive Edinburgh Waverley **Fri 11:31**; no luggage; Airbnb (accommodation address saved privately, south side) only **after 23:00**.
- Leave Saturday around **20:00**; be at Waverley by **19:30**.
- Clocks went back on 25 Oct: sunset about **16:40**. Do outdoor sights first, indoor ones later.
- Expect wind, about 8–10°C, possible rain: waterproof and grippy shoes for Arthur's Seat.
- Book today if wanted: Edinburgh Castle timed ticket and an evening ghost/vaults tour (Halloween weekend is busy).
