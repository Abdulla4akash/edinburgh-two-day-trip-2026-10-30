## Recommended route to share

**Friday:** Waverley 11:31 → quick lunch → castle around 12:30–2:30 → Victoria Street and Grassmarket → Royal Mile/St Giles' exterior → Calton Hill around 4:05 if accessible → warm break → dinner → optional ghost tour → indoor evening → Airbnb after 11pm.

**Saturday, default:** breakfast and Meadows → Dean Village at 10 → Water of Leith → Stockbridge/Circus Lane → lunch → National Museum 2–4 → Greyfriars → café/shopping → dinner at 5:30 → Waverley by 7:30 for the roughly 8pm departure.

**Saturday, hiking choice:** replace the morning through lunch with Arthur's Seat → Holyrood exteriors → Canongate lunch; rejoin the museum at 2pm. Check the current [Holyrood Park guidance](https://www.historicenvironment.scot/visit/all/holyrood-park/) before taking the hill route.

The late indoor suggestion in the README is also conditional: [Brewhemia](https://brewhemia.co.uk/about/) has event activity on 30 October, an age policy and dress requirements. Published opening until 1am does not guarantee a table or entry. No bookings have been made.
